FeaturedStatistical Analysis + Dashboard

NFL Position Value: Which Positions Are Worth Paying For?

How does salary-cap spending by position relate to team success? Across 416 team-seasons (2013 to 2025), quarterback spending has the clearest payoff, about +0.26 points per game for each point of cap share, while tight end spending fails a placebo test and looks like noise. Comes with an interactive dashboard and a written case study of the method and its limits.

FeaturedSQL Analytics + Dashboard

Telco Churn & Revenue at Risk

Answers the question a retention team actually has: which customers are churning, and how much recurring revenue is at stake? A DuckDB SQL pipeline with data-quality checks and significance tests finds one segment (month-to-month, fiber optic, electronic check) churning at 60% with $330,812 a year at risk, over 3x any other. A retention scenario shows a campaign breaks even if it saves just 8% of those customers.
